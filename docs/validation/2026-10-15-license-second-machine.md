## Automatic routing and Cloudflare-only backup expansion

The owner has only Cloudflare infrastructure. The production client now automatically performs a credential-free POST preflight, prefers Automatic or the user's saved Compatible profile, tries the other profile, then the fixed `https://license.vncode.id.vn/` backup. No arbitrary runtime endpoint is accepted. Backup identity requires a freshly generated nonce, a trusted issuer's Ed25519 signature, exact origin/product/purpose and bounded timestamp. The selected primary route is cached for 15 minutes; backup identity is refreshed after at most 60 seconds. Failure invalidates the route and briefly avoids its failed profile. Independent connection pools and IPv4-first/IPv6-retaining DNS isolate compatible routing. Short preflights are an optimization, not a new activation requirement: if none succeeds, the real operation still tries the trusted primary with its full time budget, using the other primary profile after a reported failure. The unverified backup never receives credentials. Actual transport failures do not fabricate an account denial or clear a valid bounded offline lease.

Registration/status, activation and renewal recover at most once from transport failures or generic 502/503/504. Activation and renewal restart from a new challenge and sign it with the same persisted device identity. Known account/device denial, malformed lease, signing misconfiguration and rate limits are not silently retried or bypassed. Sent business POSTs stay one-shot. A real Worker/D1/Java contract drops the response after the database redeems the code, then confirms automatic recovery uses two consumed challenges, one device and one redemption with unchanged expiry. Separate two-device/third-device-denial tests remain in place.

Portal 0.3.6 adds only a public bounded nonce/origin issuer proof API. Gateway `vncode-license-gateway` uses a same-account Service Binding to `vncode-portal`; it has no separate signing secret, DB or admin routes. Deployment instructions retain existing database/keys and add a custom domain. No live deployment or WAF changes were performed. Both services use Cloudflare; this cannot cover a full Cloudflare outage or an ISP blocking all its routes.
