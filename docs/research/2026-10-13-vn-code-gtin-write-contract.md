WB `POST /content/v2/cards/update` explicitly permits adding size SKUs and prohibits changing or deleting existing SKUs. VNCode therefore enables **ADD**, while replacement remains unavailable. The update overwrites the card and its documents: every existing size, barcode, writable characteristic, dimensions, marking confirmation, text and permit is preserved from a fresh exact nmID/chrtID read. Read-only verdict, timestamps, photos/video/tags and dimension validation fields are excluded from the write contract. The reviewed fingerprint includes every preserved writable field and shop credential scope. A changed card blocks the request. WB allows at most 3,000 cards/10 MiB, 10 requests/minute; the existing shared content limiter is reused. HTTP 200 is pending until exact owned-size readback confirms the additional GTIN and all old barcodes.

Ozon has a dedicated `POST /v1/barcode/add` operation with `barcodes:[{barcode,sku}]`. It adds a barcode without importing or overwriting product metadata. The SKU must be read from the exact product_id/offer_id, rather than assuming product_id is a SKU. One product can have at most 100 barcodes, a request at most 100 pairs, and this method is limited to 20 requests/minute per seller account. A shared three-second per-shop barcode limiter applies independently of ordinary catalog reads. `POST /v1/barcode/delete` is a separate operation with stock/six-month restrictions; the current one-send GTIN job does not implement a two-phase replacement. Ozon replacement remains unavailable. Readback must preserve the old barcode set and the original product/offer/SKU identity.

Both adapters require a fresh published trade-unit observation from the authenticated owner registry. Production `NationalCatalogGtinSource` verifies the actual CryptoPro CMS certificate before authentication and binds each source observation to the enterprise INN, tested certificate fingerprint, True API environment and shop credentials. It checks this scope before every read and after the complete refresh. The existing source field stores only `National Catalog@<SHA-256>`; older generic observations remain readable and require a new sync before authorizing writes. Unknown publication or packaging level remains ineligible. Seller writes and local candidate activation require the verified HTTPS production origin `markirovka.crpt.ru`; sandbox and arbitrary registry observations cannot authorize a production barcode update.

The durable coordinator claims SENDING before one mutation. A transport timeout reconciles and never replays. A license, identity or proof failure before the HTTP dispatch boundary is known unsent and pauses the queued job. The production transport invokes the final guard after rate-limit and shared-request-budget waits. Synthetic tests cover whole-card preservation, exact SKU binding, leading-zero conflicts, readback, response loss, partial/foreign responses and revocation during waits. No live seller mutation is used as acceptance evidence.

This covers known app-created cards across categories without claiming discovery of every external footwear card.

Native Ozon registration keeps product_id, offer_id and SKU distinct. It reviews the fresh product revision, eligible category, literal mandatory attribute types and one current product/default permit before allocation. Prepared category choices reuse one authenticated session. Allocation/feed/signing checkpoints precede one request; ambiguous transitions need readback, while proven unsent feed/signature failures retain an explicit resume.
